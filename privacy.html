Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. Overview

Station Command is a privately operated AI-assisted business operations platform under development. It is intended to support e-commerce, marketing, content planning, analytics, and authorized third-party integrations.

2. Information Accessed

When authorized by the account owner, Station Command may access Pinterest account identifiers, Pins, boards, content metadata, and available analytics. Access is limited by the permissions granted through the connected platform.

3. Use of Information

Authorized information may be used to display account information, analyze content performance, prepare marketing recommendations, and support content planning. Future publishing features are intended to require operator approval.

4. AI Processing

Station Command uses AI-assisted components. Depending on the enabled configuration, authorized information may be processed by local models or external AI providers.

5. Storage and Security

Connected-account credentials and retrieved data require appropriate access controls. Production integrations will not be enabled until credential handling and relevant safeguards have been reviewed.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Data retention and deletion procedures will be established before production integrations are activated. Account access can be revoked through the connected platform's authorization controls.

7. Third-Party Services

Station Command may integrate with services such as Pinterest and Shopify. These services maintain separate privacy policies and terms.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated as the application's functionality and data-handling practices evolve.
